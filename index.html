Design concept · Clinician grading

Where the model went wrong

A grading screen for clinicians, built on what Alterra's founder says models lack: knowing when not to act.

Gaurav Basantani
gauravbasantani.com
1The problem

AI agents can work a health record, but they don't know when not to act. Some even write the right step in a note and never do it.

2The idea

Let a clinician replay the agent's run step by step, mark where its judgment failed, and turn each mark into a scoring rule.

3What changes

AI labs get a precise record of where and why the model failed, and a rule to train against.
